# Cross-Sector Target Contracts

- **SCMS Target**: Delay_Days > 0 (PO Sent to Delivery)
- **DataCo Target**: Real shipping days > Scheduled shipping days.
- **Olist Target**: Delivered customer date > Estimated delivery date.

*Note*: These targets are functionally equivalent in defining "late", but operate on entirely different business scales (pharma logistics vs next-day e-commerce).
