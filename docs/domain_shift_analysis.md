# Anticipated Cross-Domain Shift — Protocol Note

> **Evidence status: HYPOTHESIS / NOT EXTERNALLY VALIDATED**

The DataCo and Olist adapters suggest several *plausible* transfer risks, but this repository does not claim that those shifts have been empirically quantified:

- **Feature/ontology shift:** Olist and SCMS do not expose the same logistics fields or categorical semantics.
- **Target/prevalence shift:** late-delivery prevalence may differ materially by domain and must be measured on the real target cohort.
- **Semantic shift:** labels such as shipping mode, vendor/seller, and fulfillment channel are not automatically equivalent across B2B public-health logistics and B2C e-commerce.

These are reasons to run a formal external-validation study, not results of one. See `docs/cross_sector_results.md`.
